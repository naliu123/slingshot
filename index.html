A CRASH COURSE

Give it your best shot.

You have 4 birds to pop all 3 green pigs. A good hit can set off a very satisfying chain reaction.

  1. Grab & pull. Drag the red bird backward. Pull farther for more power; move down for a higher shot.
  2. Pick your path. The short dotted arc previews the start of your flight. Try the wooden supports.
  3. Let go. Falling blocks can knock out pigs. Heavy rubble crushes them, and blocks pressing from opposite sides can squeeze them out. Watch their health bars! Your next bird loads automatically.
